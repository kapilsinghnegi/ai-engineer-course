## Round 1 — ask_search_llm
**Task:** Find the official height in metres of the Eiffel Tower and the Burj Khalifa. Provide the values and the source URLs.

**Answer:**
**Eiffel Tower**  
- Official height: **330 metres** (to the tip of its broadcast antennas)  
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eiffel_Tower  

**Burj Khalifa**  
- Official height: **828 metres** (to the architectural tip)  
- Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/burj-khalifa-is-unveiled-to-the-world-and-its-official-height-is-828-metres-80657972.html
